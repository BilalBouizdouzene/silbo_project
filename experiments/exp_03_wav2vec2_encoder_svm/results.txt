Experiment: wav2vec2 Encoder + SVM
==================================

Description:
This experiment uses wav2vec2 as a frozen feature extractor. For each audio file, the hidden states are mean-pooled over time to obtain one fixed-size embedding. A Support Vector Machine is then trained on top of these embeddings for whistled sentence classification.

Embedding extraction:
- wav2vec2 model: facebook/wav2vec2-base-960h
- Sample rate: 16000 Hz
- Embedding dimension: 768
- Pooling: mean pooling over encoder time frames

Cross-validation:
- StratifiedKFold with n_splits = 2
- Classes with fewer than 2 samples are excluded from evaluation
